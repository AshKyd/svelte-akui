# Style Guide: Cosy Fantasy Content

All placeholder content should follow a **Cosy Fantasy** theme, focusing on the whimsical, low-stakes everyday lives of magical beings like gnomes, witches, and napping dragons. Maintain a gentle, warm tone with a touch of kind irony—such as a divination group failing to predict a fire—while emphasizing pastoral magic, community-led initiatives, and domestic comforts.
